B. Touraeff, “The Volume of the Truncated Pyramid in Egyptian Mathematics”,
Ancient Egypt (1917), pp. 100–102.

Digitized volume: Internet Archive in.ernet.dli.2015.107209, supplied by the
Digital Library of India. These are complete printed pages, rendered without
retouching from 2015.107209.Ancient-Egypt-Vol4.pdf, PDF pages 115–117
(zero-based Archive leaves 114–116). Page images are not newly photographed
museum objects. The photograph printed on p. 101 has severe tonal loss in this
digitization; the typeset transcription on p. 100 is a separate editorial witness.

Original 1917 publication: public domain. Touraeff (Boris Turaev) died in 1920.
The paper is a preliminary publication, earlier than Struve's complete 1930
edition. Its column counts and numbering are retained as historical statements,
not used to identify the modern problem number. Its “empirically” is the
author's conjecture about derivation, not a fact established by the papyrus.

The author’s English translation, numerical example, hieroglyphic transcription,
photograph, comments and the bracketed editor's note have distinct roles.
The interactive dissection and all generalized algebra are modern teaching
material by Anton Soloviev (2026), licensed MIT. No dissection is attested by
these pages. “Problem 14” is the later conventional numbering.
