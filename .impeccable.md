## Design Context

### Users

English-speaking learners investigating ancient geometry and its written evidence.
The owner's brief asks for unusual, substantial learning tools with diagrams and
language, rather than a generic collection of facts. Readers should be able to
perform an operation, question it and inspect its source.

### Brand Personality

Measured, inquisitive, concrete. Explain what an experiment establishes in plain
English. Keep ancient instructions and modern explanations visibly attributable.

### Aesthetic Direction

Continue Seked's paper, ink, red and blue drawing-desk identity and its locally
served serif/sans pairing. Give the solid and its measurements the largest space.
Use ruled calculations, annotated drawings and printed evidence. Avoid dashboard
cards, decorative metrics, gradients and stock antiquity imagery.

### Design Principles

1. Let the reader manipulate the mathematical object and see the consequences.
2. Preserve real proportions and distinguish lengths, areas and volumes.
3. Put the written instruction close to the operation it actually supplies.
4. Make every operation available at phone width and through the keyboard.
5. Save a complete, illustrated reading that remains useful without the app.
